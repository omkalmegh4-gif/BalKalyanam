About BalKalyanam

Dedicated to providing love, stability, and education to orphaned and underprivileged children.

OUR FOUNDATION

Sada Shanti Balgruh (Bal Kalyan Orphanage Home)

Amravati, Maharashtra - 444601

BalKalyanam represents the dedicated public welfare initiative supporting Sada Shanti Balgruh, a caring residential home for orphaned, abandoned, and destitute children. Founded with the sacred belief that every child deserves compassion, wholesome meals, clean clothing, and access to good education, the home provides a safe, nurturing family environment where children flourish into confident adults.

Our Vision

A world where every orphaned and vulnerable child is sheltered from hardship and empowered with knowledge, character, and opportunities to realize their dreams.

Our Mission

To deliver uncompromised residential care, holistic educational guidance, healthcare, and emotional stability through transparent community participation.
