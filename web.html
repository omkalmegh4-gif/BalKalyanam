🏢 About Us

Learn about BalKalyanam and Sada Shanti Balgruh.

🌱 Overview

BalKalyanam is a social-support platform created to connect donors and volunteers with Sada Shanti Balgruh. The purpose is to make support more organized, transparent and useful.

🎯 Our Mission

  • Connect supporters with Sada Shanti.
  • Encourage need-based donations.
  • Support education and basic requirements.
  • Encourage volunteering.
  • Build community participation.

👁️ Our Vision

To create a caring community where children receive the resources, education, encouragement and opportunities they need for a brighter future.

💚 Why BalKalyanam?

Instead of making donations without knowing the actual requirements, BalKalyanam helps supporters choose specific categories such as food, money, grocery, stationery, study materials and clothes.
